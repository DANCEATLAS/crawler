# THE ULTIMATE TEACHER — how the best online dance teachers teach

*Built by the machine from 7 tutorial lessons across 6 dances and 5 dance families (studied: 8 videos; 1 were not lessons; 0 could not be read). Updated 2026-11-03. It grows every day. The chapter scripts in `episodes/` follow it. See `MOVIE_PHASE.md`.*

## The lesson skeleton the best teachers share

1. **welcome and what you will learn**
2. **where the dance comes from**
3. **the whole step at full speed, once**
4. **breakdown, one movement at a time**
5. **slow practice**
6. **practice with the music, full tempo**
7. **common mistakes and corrections**

## How they count

Most common: **5-6-7-8** — also: 1-3-5-7 with and-counts (and-1, and-3, and-5, and-7) (1); 1-2-3-4-5-6 (1); 4-1-2-3-4 (1); 12-1-2-3 / 12-1-2-3-4-5-6 (1)

## How they demonstrate

- views used by most teachers: front, mirror, back
- tempo progression: slow → full

## The cues they repeat

- and one and three and five and seven
- keep your feet together
- relax your knees
- punch to the side
- pull your head down
- pivot them on your elbows
- and out and out and in and in
- double double
- and cross and cross
- right slap left slap
- side replace close
- rock replace side

## Images they use

- imagine you are like a doll and it's a plastic doll
- <like you're pulling a string>
- It's like riding a horse
- protecting your knees and your back so you're in a spring
- you're a beautiful angel
- angel halo
- tap national anthem
- Pulling a bra strap in the front up

## Mistakes they name most

- doing squats instead of small forward and back movements
- making big upper body movements
- bringing upper body too much back
- <not moving your head with the arm movement>
- bringing ankles too close incorrectly
- arm too far behind yourself
- arm too behind follower
- too late with the under arm turn
- making steps too big instead of using rhythm
- being too upright during stomp-off instead of using body movement
- making the kick too big instead of letting the movement do the work
- lifting the shoulders while lifting the rib cage

## Body and safety advice

- relax your knees
- stay on your knees
- your body position needs to be here protecting your knees and your back so you're in a spring
- if your body starts to hurt, especially your lower back, stop

## What makes a lesson absorbable

- clear mirror angle showing both front and back view in reflection
- detailed breakdown of upper and lower body coordination
- <detailed descriptions of arm and head coordination>
- clear progressive tempo buildup from slow to fast
- showing both front and back views for learning
- clear separation of leader and follower footwork
- step-by-step numbering and titles on screen
- visual corrections for common mistakes
- breaking down each move incrementally
- visualizing correct versus incorrect technique

## What gets in the way

- long verbal explanations before full speed practice
- <some camera angles are distant>
- no mirrors or multiple students to follow along with
- rapid pace moving through many steps
- lack of back or side angle demonstrations during the footwork breakdown
- the fast pace of the music practice might be challenging for some
- camera angle faces away from the mirror, showing only the back of the teacher

## By dance family

**folk-circle** (2 lessons): counting 5-6-7-8; order: welcome and what you will learn → warm-up → where the dance comes from → breakdown, one movement at a time → slow practice → practice with the music, full tempo; cues: and out and out and in and in, double double, and cross and cross, right slap left slap, keep those abs pulled in
**african-diaspora** (2 lessons): counting 5-6-7-8; order: welcome and what you will learn → where the dance comes from → breakdown, one movement at a time → adding the next layer (arms, turns, partner) → the whole step at full speed, once; cues: 5-6-7-8-hold, lets build it up, now put them together, stomp spank step, stomp spank ball change
**street-urban** (1 lesson): counting 1-2-3-4-5-6; order: welcome and what you will learn → the whole step at full speed, once → breakdown, one movement at a time → practice with the music, full tempo; cues: punch to the side, pull your head down, pivot them on your elbows
**latin-caribbean** (1 lesson): counting 1-3-5-7 with and-counts (and-1, and-3, and-5, and-7); order: the whole step at full speed, once → welcome and what you will learn → breakdown, one movement at a time; cues: and one and three and five and seven, keep your feet together, relax your knees
**ballroom-partner** (1 lesson): counting 4-1-2-3-4; order: welcome and what you will learn → the whole step at full speed, once → where the dance comes from → breakdown, one movement at a time → common mistakes and corrections; cues: side replace close, rock replace side

## Method

Each lesson is watched once by a vision-language model (first eight minutes, public YouTube URL, nothing downloaded) and reduced to a fixed form: structure with timestamps, counting, cues, demonstration, mistakes, safety, steps with counts, absorbability, verbatim quotes. The lists above are frequencies across all lessons; a line appears only when at least one teacher did it. Per-video records: `pedagogy/videos/`. Aggregate: `pedagogy/teaching_patterns.json`.
