# Dance Atlas

Research, teaching preparation and content infrastructure for [Dancing Atlas](https://dancingatlas.lovable.app): an [Atlas](https://dancingatlas.lovable.app/atlas) of traditions and a [Viral](https://dancingatlas.lovable.app/viral) collection of challenges and dance culture.

The destination is **BDF — Best Dance Friend**, a lifelong multilingual dance mentor responsive to voice and movement. It connects learning, music/history discovery, creative video collaboration, global community and future AR experiences. Today the mission is to grow reliable content and reusable assets economically. The complete interactive product is not released or validated.
