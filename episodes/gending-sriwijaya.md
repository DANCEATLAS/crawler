NARRATION: Gending Sriwijaya was created between 1943 and 1944 during the Japanese occupation era to meet a government request to the Information Bureau (Hatching) for a dance and song to welcome official visitors to the Palembang Residency in South Sumatra, Indonesia.
NARRATION: The dance choreography was developed by Tina Haji Gong and Sukadan A. Rozak, who drew upon existing traditional Palembang dance elements.
