## Three connected loops

1. **Every existing daily pipeline run:** reassess every current Atlas/Viral dossier against current evidence, reviews and permissions. New external questions with exact typed profile IDs enter their dossiers and the appropriate research/video/rights/review/platform queues. New questions change the approval fingerprint. A candidate question is not an approved teaching fact.
2. **Every existing profile research call:** investigate requirements beyond today's fields. Return at most four source-linked questions with a reason and the next evidence/experiment needed. No extra model call, raised token cap or raised research budget. Model generation is currently paused by spending_policy.json; the queue remains intact until an explicit dated allowance exists. Questions requiring more research enter the existing completion allowance (at most one third of enrichment, capped at five), with the existing seven-day cooldown. Prioritize these questions ahead of legacy checklist hints. Once the requirements pass has run, unchanged evidence skips repeat composition. Other lanes remain explicit work for the relevant researcher/reviewer; they are not silently converted to more text-generation calls.
3. **Weekly external research:** the existing ChatGPT task **Dance Atlas readiness research** runs Sunday at 16:00 Asia/Tokyo, reduced from daily on 14 November to control plan usage. Two successful research passes on 12–13 November supplied learning/accessibility and music requirements; their exact findings are retained in the register and dated journals. Each pass selects the oldest-reviewed area and at most two contrasting profiles and six external pages. It consumes ChatGPT plan capacity, has no API spending authority, and can continue public research if authenticated writes are unavailable. Unchanged sources need not be reread before their review date unless new evidence warrants it.

The seven rotating areas are movement/teaching; learner outcomes/accessibility; music/rights; AI interaction/memory; human BDF relationships/groups/synchronization; Viral creation/distribution; technology/devices/economics. Include minority variants, underrepresented languages, caregiver-led and later-life use. Investigate enjoyment, comprehension, retention and social belonging through appropriate evidence; attractive generated output is insufficient.

## What a research pass must produce

Ask: what must the learner actually be able to do; what is unknown or untested; what could disprove readiness; what new method changes the problem; which profiles/modes are affected; and what is the cheapest adequate next test?

Record each material finding with source/date/location, observed fact versus inference, exact unresolved question, affected profiles or justified platform scope, proposed field or benchmark, next evidence/action, acceptance criterion, cost constraint and recheck date. Compare competing methods and contradictory evidence. A provider demonstration does not establish Dance Atlas performance. Human reviews, rights and actual learning trials cannot be self-certified by the model.

Keep `pipeline_state/readiness_findings.json` as the external findings register. `pipeline/readiness_research.py` validates/routes questions and generates `pipeline_state/readiness_research_agenda.json` and `CONTINUOUS_RESEARCH_REPORT.md` during activation. These are an agenda and evidence register, not a web-research engine. The composer and scheduled research task perform the research.

Each finding has `id`, `domain` (research/video/rights/review/platform), `question`, `why_needed`, `next_evidence`, `source` (HTTPS), `source_location`, `checked_on`, `recheck_on`, `inference`, `status`, and `profile_ids` (exact `atlas:<slug>` or `viral:<slug>` values). Platform-wide investigations use `scope: platform`; do not attach speculative vendor limitations to every dance. Questions learned within a profile research call use the same first five descriptive fields and only sources supplied to that call. Keep private learner data, contracts and contacts outside this public register.

Close a finding only with `status: resolved` and `resolution: {evidence, reviewer, reviewed_on, recheck_on}` documenting actual resolution. Bare closed flags and future-dated reviews do not remove it; expired closures reopen for review. These metadata checks do not prove a review's truth or replace the activation approval gates. Applicability and any change to the standard need evidence-based editorial review. Never loosen quality merely to improve counts.

Update `area_reviews[area].checked_on` and `profile_reviews[typed_id] = {checked_on, fingerprint}` only after an actual pass. Update `last_external_research` only after real source inspection. Running the offline agenda must never advance these research dates. Keep dated journal notes with source findings and failed approaches so the same investigation is not repeatedly funded.
