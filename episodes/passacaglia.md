### 2. Where and when (70 s) · `where`
NARRATION: The passacaglia originated in early 17th-century Spain as a strummed guitar interlude between instrumentally accompanied dances or songs, derived from the words pasar (cross, pass) and calle (street).
NARRATION: The first written examples appear in an Italian source dated 1606.
NARRATION: Girolamo Frescobaldi redefined it in the late 1620s into continuous variations over a bass ostinato.
