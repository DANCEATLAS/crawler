Costume notes: Costumes and makeup in Krishnanattam are handcrafted by the teachers and artists. Male characters wear a starched gathered petticoat beneath a white skirt featuring horizontal orange/orange-red and black stripes near the bottom. Their upper dress consists of a long-sleeved shirt open at the back, a wooden breast-plate, chest ornaments of beads and fresh flora, upper arm bands, wrist ornaments, a girdle, and shawls fitted with mirrors at each end. Female roles wear long floor-length gathered white skirts with red borders, back-tied long-sleeved blouses, wooden breast-plates covered in red cloth, and ankle bells (chilanka). Face makeup utilizes three basic colors: pazhuppu (red-dominant orange), minukku (yellow-dominant orange), and paccha (yellow-green). A chutti—two white protruding borders along the jawline—frames paccha make-up. The most sacred garment is the headgear, which comes in two primary forms: a bun (mudi) and a crown (kiriitam) topped with peacock feathers.
