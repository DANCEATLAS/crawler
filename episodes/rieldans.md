NARRATION: Originally danced by Khoisan hunters around campfires after hunting expeditions or harvests, it absorbed distinct Irish and Scottish folk music influences alongside Dutch folk songs.
NARRATION: Over time, the native Khoisan languages mostly disappeared, and the Afrikaans word 'riel' was borrowed from the Scottish 'reel'.
