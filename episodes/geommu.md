### 5. Listen (70 s) · `music`
NARRATION: Geommu is accompanied by Samhyeon-nyukgak, a traditional six-instrument ensemble comprising two piri (bamboo double-reed oboes), one daegeum (large bamboo transverse flute), one haegeum (two-string bowed fiddle), one janggu (double-headed hourglass drum), and one buk (barrel drum).
NARRATION: The janggu uses different leather skins on each head to produce distinct pitches, while the buk is struck with an open hand and a wooden stick.
NARRATION: Listen for the Piri (2), Daegeum, Haegeum, Janggu.
NARRATION: The pulse is Varied changdan meters at about 100 beats a minute.
NARRATION: A wind-and-percussion soundscape dominated by the resonant bamboo reeding of twin piri and the breathy transverse tones of the daegeum, underpinned by the dual-pitched skin tones of the janggu drum, punctuated by the metallic clinking of sword rings.
