# THE ULTIMATE TEACHER — how the best online dance teachers teach

*Built by the machine from 203 tutorial lessons across 128 dances and 8 dance families (studied: 230 videos; 26 were not lessons; 1 could not be read). Updated 2026-11-21. It grows every day. The chapter scripts in `episodes/` follow it. See `MOVIE_PHASE.md`.*

## The lesson skeleton the best teachers share

1. **welcome and what you will learn**
2. **where the dance comes from**
3. **warm-up**
4. **the whole step at full speed, once**
5. **breakdown, one movement at a time**
6. **slow practice**
7. **practice with the music, full tempo**
8. **recap and homework**

## How they count

Most common: **5-6-7-8** — also: 1-2-3-4-5-6-7-8 (23); 1-2-3-4 (13); 1-2 (6); 1-2-3 (5)

## How they demonstrate

- views used by most teachers: front, side, back
- tempo progression: slow → half → full
- repetitions per step: about 4

## Mistakes they name most

- not lifting heels high enough
- not using the whole foot for the step
- not using weight transfer
- changing height during the sit movement
- bouncing hands up and down
- getting the partner out of alignment and balance during turns
- moving too much or balancing outside the spot instead of keeping everything in place
- not pointing feet
- not engaging core and glutes
- lifting the skirt too high or going too diagonal
- not maintaining skirt line in the center
- doing squats instead of small forward and back movements

## Body and safety advice

- none
- do not press hard on the hand connection because that can hurt
- prepare safety mats
- aerial is a dangerous activity
- do not attempt alone without consulting professionals
- make sure there is a good space between your head and the ground
- relax your knees
- stay on your knees

## What makes a lesson absorbable

- clear verbal cues
- demonstration from multiple angles
- slow-motion demonstrations
- clear verbal instructions
- step-by-step breakdown
- repetition of steps
- clear visual demonstrations from multiple angles
- clear step-by-step breakdown
- the teacher repeats the steps multiple times
- demonstrating from multiple angles

## What gets in the way

- none noted
- limited camera angles
- fast transitions between steps
- no verbal breakdown or explanation of steps
- no multi-angle demonstration
- lack of explicit verbal counting
- only a single back-angle view is shown during the breakdown, lacking a mirrored front view
- lack of mirror-image view or alternate camera angles

## Method

Each lesson is watched once by a vision-language model (first eight minutes, public YouTube URL, nothing downloaded) and reduced to a fixed form: structure with timestamps, counting, cues, demonstration, mistakes, safety, steps with counts, absorbability, verbatim quotes. The lists above are frequencies across all lessons; a line appears only when at least one teacher did it. Per-video records: `pedagogy/videos/`. Aggregate: `pedagogy/teaching_patterns.json`.
