NARRATION: The Phulpati dance originated in the Malwa region of Madhya Pradesh in central India.
NARRATION: Developed specifically within rural and semi-rural communities, the tradition emerged as a designated cultural expression for unmarried women, granting them a distinct role in seasonal celebrations.
NARRATION: The dance is tightly linked with major festive cycles, including the spring festival of Holi and the fifteen-day Dashain festival observed in the region.

NARRATION: The primary occasion for the Phulpati dance is the festival of Holi, along with the auspicious seventh day—Phulpati—of the fifteen-day Dashain festival in Madhya Pradesh.
