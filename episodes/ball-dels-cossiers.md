### 2. Where and when (70 s) · `where`
NARRATION: The Ball dels Cossiers arrived on Majorca from Catalonia in the 14th century during the Aragonian conquest under King Jaime I, with the earliest manuscript evidence recorded in Sóller in 1544.
NARRATION: While some scholars trace its roots to indigenous pagan nature spirit rituals, the form became integrated into Catholic festivals like Corpus Christi.
NARRATION: Franco's 20th-century political repression nearly extinguished the tradition until film director José María Oliveira spearheaded its revival in 1962 at a Palma hotel.
