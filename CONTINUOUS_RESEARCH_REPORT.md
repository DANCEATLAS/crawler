## Current platform investigations

- Can targeted inspection recover essential teaching beyond the first eight minutes and fast transitions within the existing budget?
  Evidence to obtain: Compare timestamped practitioner labels with the existing extractor and a candidate targeted-clip method. Measure missed critical moments, timing errors, total tokens and failures before approving a change. No paid trial is authorized.
  Source: https://aistudio.google.com/docs/video-understanding
- Can multi-person feedback keep each partner's identity and role through crossings, occlusion and tracking loss?
  Evidence to obtain: Prepare a consented partner/group test with crossing paths, matching clothing, partial occlusion and re-entry. Measure identity swaps, incorrect corrections, abstention and recovery; define acceptable outcomes with practitioners before activation.
  Source: https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker?tab=Web
- Can learners retain the declared skill after instructional prompts fade, while keeping access supports and culturally essential musical cues?
  Evidence to obtain: First draft a unit-level rubric and cue inventory offline. When an authorized practitioner and consenting learners are available, compare equal-practice continuous prompting with gradually reduced or learner-requested prompting. Record immediate and delayed performance with instructional prompts absent, access supports retained, and one reviewed transfer condition. No recruitment or trial occurred.
  Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7644831/
- Can a learner perform an approved movement translation without being corrected back toward an unsuitable reference body?
  Evidence to obtain: Draft a movement-intent contract and reviewer-labelled cases: accepted translation, meaningful timing/intent error, and unobservable detail. Bind the correction rubric to the selected reviewed version. An inclusive practitioner and the relevant cultural authority must assess applicability before real instruction.
  Source: https://www.stopgapdance.com/inclusive-choreography/
- Can command gestures be disabled independently of movement feedback, so dancing cannot accidentally skip, record, invite or publish?
  Evidence to obtain: Specify separate command-gesture and movement-feedback states, conventional/assistive controls, and deliberate arming for optional gestures. At a future local prototype, replay the same event sequence with commands enabled/disabled and verify alternative pause/repeat/exit controls.
  Source: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/motion-actuation.html
- Can a properly licensed recording actually reach the intended learner export destination without unresolved automated claims, including exports from learners’ own channels?
  Evidence to obtain: Desk-audit one future cleared asset against intended destination, account ownership, duration and claimant chain. Record claim-resolution responsibility and costs. Any later private upload test requires separate explicit upload authorization; none was performed.
  Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6070344?hl=en
- After slowing, looping or changing speed, do musical accents and lesson cues remain correctly aligned to the exact recording version?
  Evidence to obtain: Draft source-clock/output-clock fixtures for pause, seek, loop and rate changes. Then, only with permission-cleared audio, compare renderer outputs at musician-approved rates and loop boundaries on supported devices, including percussive and vocal material.
  Source: https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio/#playback-AudioBufferSourceNode
