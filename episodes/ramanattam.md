### 7. The people (60 s) · `people`
NARRATION: Key historic figures associated with Ramanattam include Veera Kerala Varma (Kottarakkara Thampuran, AD 1653–1694), who authored the plays and founded the art form; King Manavedan (Zamorin of Kozhikode), whose refusal to share Krishnattam sparked its creation; and Kottayath Thampuran, who later adapted Ramanattam into Kathakali.
NARRATION: Ramanattam originated in Kottarakkara in southern Kerala and heavily influenced the development of Kathakali at the Mridangashaileshwari temple in Muzhakkunn, northern Kerala.
NARRATION: Derived as a direct response to Krishnattam (created by Zamorin Raja of Calicut in 1657 AD), Ramanattam served as the foundational parent tradition that evolved into Kathakali under the development of Kottayath Thampuran.
