### 7. The people (60 s) · `people`
NARRATION: The skipari, or lead singer, is a central figure in the dance, responsible for knowing and singing all verses of the kvæði.
NARRATION: Several Faroese dance associations have been instrumental in preserving and promoting the dance, including Tvøroyrar Dansifelag (founded 1983, originally 1958), Sumbiar Dansifelag (1966), and Dansifelagið í Havn (1952).
NARRATION: Three main variants exist: the common stígingarstev, characterized by slow leftward stepping; the trokingarstev, where dancers stand mostly still or move backward during verses and step forward during refrains, more common in southern islands like Suðuroy; and the bandadansur or ribbon dance, involving two rows holding ribbons and passing under raised ribbons during refrains.
NARRATION: The Faroese chain dance is a direct descendant of medieval circle dances, preserved uniquely in the Faroe Islands.
