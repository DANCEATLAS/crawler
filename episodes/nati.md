### 6. The costume and the setting (60 s) · `costume`
NARRATION: Costumes vary by district across Himachal Pradesh.
NARRATION: Male attire includes Himachali topis (caps), fitted woollen suthan trousers, kurtas, Nehru jackets, and gachi waist sashes, with Kinnaur dancers adorning topis with flowers and Indian trumpet seeds, Sirmaur men wearing choltu tunics, and Kullu men wearing flower garland headwear.
