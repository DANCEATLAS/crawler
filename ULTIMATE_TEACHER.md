# THE ULTIMATE TEACHER — how the best online dance teachers teach

*Built by the machine from 2 tutorial lessons across 2 dances and 2 dance families (studied: 2 videos; 0 were not lessons; 0 could not be read). Updated 2026-11-03. It grows every day. The chapter scripts in `episodes/` follow it. See `MOVIE_PHASE.md`.*

## The lesson skeleton the best teachers share

1. **the whole step at full speed, once**
2. **welcome and what you will learn**
3. **where the dance comes from**
4. **breakdown, one movement at a time**

## How they count

Most common: **1-3-5-7 with and-counts (and-1, and-3, and-5, and-7)** — also: 1-2-3-4 (1)

## How they demonstrate

- views used by most teachers: front, mirror, back
- tempo progression: slow → full

## The cues they repeat

- and one and three and five and seven
- keep your feet together
- relax your knees
- stomp spank step
- stomp spank ball change
- step toe step hop step hop out in
- stomp heel stomp heel

## Images they use

- imagine you are like a doll and it's a plastic doll

## Mistakes they name most

- doing squats instead of small forward and back movements
- making big upper body movements
- bringing upper body too much back
- doing a big full foot instead of a stomp
- falling into the wrong rhythm on the break

## Body and safety advice

- relax your knees
- stay on your knees

## What makes a lesson absorbable

- clear mirror angle showing both front and back view in reflection
- detailed breakdown of upper and lower body coordination
- clear counting and repetition of each step
- demonstrating both right and left sides separately

## What gets in the way

- long verbal explanations before full speed practice
- camera angle faces away from the mirror, showing only the back of the teacher

## Method

Each lesson is watched once by a vision-language model (first eight minutes, public YouTube URL, nothing downloaded) and reduced to a fixed form: structure with timestamps, counting, cues, demonstration, mistakes, safety, steps with counts, absorbability, verbatim quotes. The lists above are frequencies across all lessons; a line appears only when at least one teacher did it. Per-video records: `pedagogy/videos/`. Aggregate: `pedagogy/teaching_patterns.json`.
