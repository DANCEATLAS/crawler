# THE ULTIMATE TEACHER — how the best online dance teachers teach

*Built by the machine from 170 tutorial lessons across 108 dances and 8 dance families (studied: 191 videos; 20 were not lessons; 1 could not be read). Updated 2026-11-20. It grows every day. The chapter scripts in `episodes/` follow it. See `MOVIE_PHASE.md`.*

## The lesson skeleton the best teachers share

1. **welcome and what you will learn**
2. **where the dance comes from**
3. **warm-up**
4. **the whole step at full speed, once**
5. **breakdown, one movement at a time**
6. **slow practice**
7. **practice with the music, full tempo**
8. **recap and homework**

## How they count

Most common: **5-6-7-8** — also: 1-2-3-4-5-6-7-8 (18); 1-2-3-4 (12); 1-2 (5); 1-2-3 (4)

## How they demonstrate

- views used by most teachers: front, side, back
- tempo progression: slow → half → full
- repetitions per step: about 4

## Mistakes they name most

- lifting the shoulders while lifting the rib cage
- bending over while opening the rib cage
- not preloading or forcing weight downward correctly
- rolling in (weight shifting forward)
- climbing up looking uncleaned if legs muscle not engaged
- the teacher warns against doing a 100% choreographed routine, as it can cause the dancer to lose the feeling of the music
- moving shoulders or upper body instead of keeping it still
- boxing or trying to fight someone with arms
- not having a flexible back
- not having enough momentum
- weight in the wrong part of the foot
- slouching or leading forward

## Body and safety advice

- none
- if your body starts to hurt, especially your lower back, stop
- wear a helmet and protective gear
- keep weight evenly distributed on the foot
- be prepared for slight discomfort if this is your first time having silks wrap around your thighs
- use a floor mat
- the teacher advises keeping the knees flexed to protect the joints and facilitate hip movement
- make sure that you have comfortable shoes before you start this stuff, especially if you're still trying to learn, because you don't want to incur some injuries

## What makes a lesson absorbable

- demonstration from multiple angles
- clear verbal instructions
- slow-motion demonstrations
- clear verbal cues
- step-by-step breakdown
- clear step-by-step breakdown
- clear visual demonstrations from multiple angles
- the teacher breaks down the steps into small, manageable parts
- the teacher repeats the steps multiple times
- clear verbal explanations

## What gets in the way

- none noted
- no verbal breakdown or explanation of steps
- fast transitions between steps
- lack of explicit verbal counting
- the fast pace of the music practice might be challenging for some
- lack of front-facing demonstration
- fast transition into full speed without intermediate tempo increments
- rapid transitions between riding clips and instructional talking

## Method

Each lesson is watched once by a vision-language model (first eight minutes, public YouTube URL, nothing downloaded) and reduced to a fixed form: structure with timestamps, counting, cues, demonstration, mistakes, safety, steps with counts, absorbability, verbatim quotes. The lists above are frequencies across all lessons; a line appears only when at least one teacher did it. Per-video records: `pedagogy/videos/`. Aggregate: `pedagogy/teaching_patterns.json`.
