NARRATION: Dance for physical exercise in China traces to ancient records such as the Lüshi Chunqiu from the third century B.C.E., which describes slow dancing during Emperor Yao's reign to alleviate joint stiffness caused by damp weather, as well as public performances during the Song dynasty.
NARRATION: Modern guangchangwu, however, originated directly in the socio-economic transformations of the late 20th century.
