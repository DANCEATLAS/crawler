### 5. Listen (70 s) · `music`
NARRATION: Music for Pukllay is provided by ensembles playing various Andean duct flutes belonging to the pinquillo family.
NARRATION: The primary melody carrier is the senqatanqana (also termed toqoro or Senka Tenkana, meaning 'the growing nose'), a massive duct flute carved from thick reed with an external duct akin to the moceño.
NARRATION: Listen for the senqatanqana (toqoro), pinquillo, cherque (straight moceño), wajra (horn trumpet).
NARRATION: A deep, penetrating sonority defined by the reedy, hollow breath of oversized senqatanqana duct flutes and buzzing cherque flutes, counterpointed by the raw brassy drone of wajra horn trumpets. Beneath the winds, an uninterrupted percussive bed is created not by hand drums, but by the physical movements of dancers—heavy thumping wooden soles, high-pitched ringing bells from wide waist belts, and the dry metallic scrape of giant iron heel spurs.
